Model: drag = ½ × air density (1.225 kg/m³) × CdA × speed²; drag power = drag × speed. The sampled inertial force is modeled mass × estimated acceleration; it is not engine output. Mass affects kinetic energy and this inertial-force estimate, not the drag formula. Acceleration is a rough estimate from adjacent sampled speed values. Wind, downforce/ride-height maps, tire effects, road grade, fuel load, actual driver mass and actual car setup are not supplied by this dataset.
