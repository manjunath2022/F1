Model per sample: drag = ½ρ(CdA)v²; downforce = ½ρ(ClA)v²; drag power = drag × v; kinetic energy = ½mv²; longitudinal acceleration = Δv/Δt; inertial force = ma. CdA, ClA, masses and air density are editable assumptions, not telemetry. Wind, DRS-dependent aero maps, ride height, tire load, road grade and fuel load are unavailable, so treat aero figures as estimates—not engine power or official car data.
